## Meet Ana

**Narrator:** Meet Ana, a resident of Mint Town. Coffee before work. Groceries for home. She checks her wallet: enough left for the week. These little payments are part of her routine. She's done this a hundred times. Then a message interrupts her morning. It says her wallet is at risk.

## The phishing message

**Narrator:** It uses the wallet's name and says she has only five minutes.

**Narrator:** The link opens a convincing copy of her wallet.

**Narrator:** Move money to a safe account. Keep it secret.

**Ana:** It looks real.

**Narrator:** Then, nine hundred pesos disappear.

## A customer needs help

**Ana:** Wait—what? My money's gone!

**Narrator:** She files a report. Then comes the wait. Days can pass with her case unresolved.

## Why it matters

**Narrator:** Her story is one of many across financial services. In six ASEAN markets, ninety-six percent worried about scams or hacking.

**Narrator:** Forty-five percent of reported cases remained unresolved.

**Narrator:** One wallet's published investigation window: two to seven days.

**Narrator:** But what if there's a faster way to resolve her case?
